# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** November 2026  
**Project:** SHAIDS – Student Hub for AI & Data Science  
**Institution:** Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science, A. C. Patil College of Engineering (ACPCE), Navi Mumbai  
**Maintainer / Author:** Vedant Mahamunkar

---

## 1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled on the **SHAIDS** web platform. SHAIDS is an informational and community portal for the Department of Artificial Intelligence & Data Science at ACPCE.

---

## 2. Information Displayed on the Platform

Public pages on the SHAIDS platform display departmental information curated by authorized administrators, including:

- Student committee member names, roles, academic years, skills, and public portfolio/social links
- Faculty names, designations, qualifications, research domains, and institutional contact details
- Departmental events, fest results, student projects, NPTEL certifications, toppers, and event photo galleries

All displayed profiles and media are published for academic and departmental showcase purposes.

---

## 3. Data Collection & Storage

- **Public Visitors:** Browsing public pages does not require account registration. We do not sell, rent, or trade visitor data, nor do we run third-party advertising trackers.
- **Browser Storage:** The platform may use standard browser storage (`localStorage` / `BroadcastChannel`) strictly for functional features such as theme preference (dark/light mode) and cross-tab content synchronization.
- **Backend Infrastructure:** Departmental content and media assets are hosted and managed via **Supabase** (PostgreSQL and Supabase Storage).
- **Admin Access:** Administrative authentication is restricted to authorized SHAIDS maintainers for managing departmental content.

---

## 4. External Links

The platform includes links to external websites (such as curated study resources, GitHub, LinkedIn, or institutional pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external third-party websites.

---

## 5. Content Updates or Removal Requests

If any student, faculty member, or contributor wishes to update or remove their profile details, contact information, or event photographs published on the platform, please contact the **SHAIDS Tech Team** or the **Department of AI & Data Science, ACPCE**.

---

## 6. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be revised periodically as the platform evolves. Any updates will be reflected directly in this document.
